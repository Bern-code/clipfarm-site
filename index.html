Clipfarm

Clipfarm turns long videos into short vertical clips with captions and uploads them to the operator's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X accounts.

Connect TikTok

How it works

Clipfarm finds the most-watched moments in a long video, cuts them into 9:16 clips, adds a title and word-by-word subtitles, and sends each finished clip to the connected account as a draft or a post. The account owner signs in with TikTok once to authorise uploads and can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings.

Clipfarm is a private tool run by a single creator for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public.
