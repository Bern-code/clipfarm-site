Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

What we collect

When you connect a TikTok account, Clipfarm receives an access token and a refresh token from TikTok, plus basic account info (such as your open ID and display name). After uploading, it records the returned video IDs and publish status.

How we use it

This data is used only to upload clips to your account and to check whether an upload succeeded. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Tokens and upload records are stored locally on the machine running Clipfarm. This website does not collect, store or track anything, and uses no cookies or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account stays connected. You can revoke Clipfarm's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security & permissions → Apps). Tokens then stop working and are deleted. To ask for deletion of any stored records, open an issue.

Third parties

Videos and metadata are sent to the platforms you connect (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X) under their own privacy policies.

Children

Clipfarm is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
